01 · About

I'm Siddhant Yadav, a developer and designer from Kathmandu. I'm not just someone who makes websites look pretty — I understand why they work, and how to make them work better.

My background is in IT, which means I don't just push pixels around in Figma. I understand the systems behind every design decision — which makes my work faster to build, easier to scale, and better thought-through.
